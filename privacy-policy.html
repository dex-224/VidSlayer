Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025
1
Overview
Welcome to VidSlayer ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website at vidslayer.com (the "Site").

VidSlayer is a free, browser-based YouTube creator toolkit. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.
2
Information We Do Not Collect
VidSlayer runs entirely in your browser. We do not:
  • Require you to create an account or sign in
  • Collect your name, email address, or any personal information
  • Store your keywords, titles, scripts, or any content you generate
  • Use any database or server-side storage
  • Sell or share your data with third parties
All content you generate stays entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us.
3
Google AdSense & Cookies
VidSlayer uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to serve ads based on your browsing history and interests.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.
4
Third-Party Services
Our site loads the following third-party resources:
  • Google Fonts — to display custom fonts (Syne, DM Mono, Roboto). Google may log font requests.
  • Google AdSense — to display advertisements. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
These services have their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them.
5
Cookies
VidSlayer itself does not set any cookies. However, Google AdSense may set cookies on your device to serve personalized ads. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings or opt out via aboutads.info.
6
Analytics
We may use Google Analytics in the future to understand general traffic patterns (e.g. how many people visit, which pages are popular). If implemented, this would involve anonymized, aggregated data only — no personally identifiable information. We will update this policy if analytics are added.
7
Children's Privacy
VidSlayer is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.
8
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
9
Your Rights
Since VidSlayer does not collect any personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. Your privacy is protected by default — no action required on your part.
